# Cold Start Behavioral Analysis (20 Students x 30 Selections)

- **Total Students Simulated**: 20
- **Selections per Student**: 30 (600 total selections)
- **First-Selected Topic across ALL 20 Cold-Start Students**: Judiciary, Rights & Doctrines (100% deterministic initial pick).
- **Multi-Iteration Behavior**: For all 20 students, by iteration 2 the system penalizes Judiciary and rotates cleanly to Executive, Federalism, Elections, and Panchayati Raj.
- **Student-to-Student Variation**: 0% variance between students under identical zero-history starting states (desirable deterministic property).
